Special Overall Prizes

  • Best Under 18

  • Best Under 12
  • Best Under 10
  • Best Over 65
  • Best female player
  • Best game

All special prizes consist of a trophy

The special overall prizes are awarded on a single overall ranking combining the three opens A, B and C; in the event of a tie, the tie-break criteria apply. Each prize goes to the best player in the relevant category; for the youth categories only the trophy of the lowest age category is awarded.

They cannot be combined with other prizes, except for the best game prize, which is always awarded at the organisers' sole discretion.
