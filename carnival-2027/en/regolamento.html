General rules

Open tournaments A, B and C are valid for FIDE standard rating changes.

Any player who arrives at the board more than 60 minutes after the start time will lose the game.

Smoking is strictly prohibited, including electronic cigarettes, with the exception of any areas specifically designated for smokers.

Players are strictly prohibited from bringing any electronic device into the playing hall.

All players must hold a valid 2027 federation membership at the start of the first round. For Opens A, B and C a competitive membership card (tessera agonistica) is required.

By entering the tournament, each player of legal age, or the legal guardian of a minor, consents to the publication of certain personal data (surname, first name, category, ELO), as well as of results, games played and images (photographs or video recordings made at the venue), on the website and social media of ASD Scacchi Valle dell'Aniene and of the FSI, and on the web in general.

By confirming entry to the tournament, each player accepts the terms of these regulations.

Entry is free only for players with ELO ≥ 2300 as at 05/02/2027.

In no case may prizes be combined.

The prize-giving ceremony is an integral part of the event: winners who are not present will forfeit their prize.

Special overall prizes

In a single overall ranking combining the three opens A, B and C, special prizes are awarded; in the event of a tie, the tie-break criteria apply. Each prize goes to the best player in the relevant category (Under 18/16/14/12/10, Over 65, best female player); for the youth categories only the trophy of the lowest age category is awarded. They cannot be combined with other prizes, except for the best game prize, which is always awarded at the organisers' sole discretion.

Half-point byes

Once only, a player may request a half-point bye from the chief arbiter in any round except the last two, as governed by art. 6.3 of the R.T.F., subject to the conditions set out in that article.

Wild cards

Should players request them, 4 wild cards will be allocated for Open A and 4 for Open B. Requests must be sent to info@scacchianiene.it. ELO will be taken into account and, secondarily, the order in which requests are received. The organisers reserve full discretion in the selection.

Pairings and tie-break

The pairing system is Swiss.

In order, the criteria used to award prizes are: REP, Buchholz Cut 1, Buchholz Total, ARO.

Tournament structure: Opens A, B and C, 5 rounds, time control 90'+30''.

POINTS NOT COVERED BY THESE REGULATIONS SHALL BE GOVERNED BY THE R.T.F. AND THE LAWS OF CHESS, AT THE ARBITER'S FINAL AND UNQUESTIONABLE JUDGEMENT
