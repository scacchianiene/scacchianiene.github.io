Wild cards. Should players request them, 4 wild cards will be allocated for Open A and 4 for Open B. Requests must be sent to info@scacchianiene.it. ELO will be taken into account and, secondarily, the order in which requests are received. The organisers reserve full discretion in the selection.

Membership. All players must hold a valid 2027 federation membership at the start of the first round. For Opens A, B and C a competitive membership card (tessera agonistica) is required.
